## Weather and air-quality data

Weather forecasts: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/). Air quality: [CAMS via Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api).

Data is attributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The HUD transforms forecast values into monochrome charts and an interpolated nine-point wind field. Moon data is calculated locally with SunCalc.
